UTruffula



The inspiration behind these plants!


When exploring the Art Building's plants, I really liked the colorful aspect of all the plants. They reminded me of the trees from The Lorax, thus forming my theme.

To the right is a visual displaying the colors from photos that I took of each plant. Below are the images I pulled colors from. Using a hex code picker website, I generated 10 different colors to display in the chart. My goal was to display the colors I thought were unique to each plant and the colors that set them apart in terms of hues. I hand arranged the colors instead of generating an algorithm to represent the colors from greens (bottom) to plant specific colors that were defined through specific features such as their petals.
